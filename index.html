Server CPUs: a brave new world

Agentic AI turned CPUs from a declining afterthought into the fastest-repriced market in the space — TAM claims escalated from $60bn (2026) to $100–200bn (2030) between February and May 2026.

Server CPU revenues by vendor

Total grows $25bn → $81bn (26% CAGR 2025-30). Arm goes from $4bn to $36bn (44% share); Intel recovers to $24bn but its share halves to 29%.
Source: CPU p.8

AI server CPU market share

Arm (via Nvidia Grace/Vera) 40% → 55% by 2030. Intel's Nvidia partnership ($5bn stake, NVLink) helps but share losses persist.
Source: CPU p.5

Server CPU shipments: AI overtakes traditional

AI server CPUs grow 43%/yr to 30m units by 2030 while traditional units recover to their 2020 peak (17m) — the CPU:GPU-die ratio doubles from 1:4 to 1:2 with standalone CPU racks.
Source: CPU p.2, p.4

The 2030 CPU TAM debate

NSR's bottom-up says $81bn; AMD claims $120bn+, Arm ~$100bn, Nvidia $200bn for Vera alone. Even the conservative case triples the market.
Sources: CPU p.8, NVDA-CPU p.1, BIBLE-1Q26 p.13

Unpacking Nvidia's $20bn CPU guide (2026)

CPU-only racks for agentic AI and reinforcement learning are ramping at a 33-53% attach rate in year one, and Nvidia instantly holds >33% of CPU revenues.
Source: NVDA-CPU p.1 (26-May-2026)

ROI & customer economics

Cloud AI earns lower returns than traditional cloud — but still healthy ones, especially over the true life of the chips. The hyperscaler P&L can absorb the depreciation wave, with one exception.

Traditional vs AI cloud server economics

Gross cash return on gross operating assets: ~50% traditional vs ~35% AI. AI cloud is more fragmented, bought in bulk, with lower switching costs — and far more capital-intensive.
Source: ROIC p.3, p.7 (What returns to expect on Cloud AI?, 28-Jan-2026)

Oracle–OpenAI 4.5GW contract: FCF profile

12% IRR over the contract (2H27–1H32, ~$300bn revenues, 30% GM) — but 20% IRR over the 10-year useful life of the chips, as post-depreciation compute costs ~70-80% less. Hover bars for revenue/GM by year.
Source: ROIC p.5, p.10
